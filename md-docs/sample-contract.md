2. TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of six (6) months, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Section 2 (the "Term"). The Agreement may be renewed for successive one-month periods upon written agreement of the Parties.

2.2 Termination by Company. Company may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, with or without cause, upon five (5) business days' written notice to Contractor. 2.3 Termination by Contractor. Contractor may terminate this Agreement only for material breach by Company that remains uncured for thirty (30) days following written notice specifying the breach in reasonable detail. Contractor shall continue to perform all Services during any cure period.

2.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement for any reason, Contractor shall immediately: (a) cease all work on the Services; (b) deliver to Company all Deliverables, whether completed or in progress, including all drafts, sketches, wireframes, prototypes, and working files; (c) return or destroy all Confidential Information; and (d) provide a written certification of compliance with this Section 2.4. All provisions of this Agreement that by their nature should survive termination shall survive, including without limitation Sections 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.

3.2 Acceptance. Company shall have thirty (30) business days following delivery of the final Deliverables to review and either accept or reject the work (the "Acceptance Period"). 3.3 Payment Terms. Approved invoices shall be paid within sixty (60) days of Company's receipt of a proper invoice. Company reserves the right to withhold payment if Contractor has failed to comply with any term of this Agreement. Late payments shall not accrue interest.

3.4 Expenses. Contractor shall bear all costs and expenses incurred in the performance of the Services, including but not limited to software licenses, equipment, internet access, travel, and any subcontractor fees. No expenses shall be reimbursable unless pre-approved in writing by Company.

3.5 Taxes. Contractor shall be solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes arising from compensation received under this Agreement, including self-employment taxes, income taxes, and any other applicable taxes or assessments. Company shall issue a Form 1099-NEC as required by law.
